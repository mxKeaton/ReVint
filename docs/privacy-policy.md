# ReVint — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-17 · Publisher: mxKeaton · Contact: https://github.com/mxKeaton/ReVint/issues_

ReVint runs entirely in your browser. It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party, and it contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.

## What ReVint accesses
- Your own Vinted pages (your member page and the sell form) on Vinted domains. When you save a listing, ReVint reads that page (title, description, price, category, size, condition, brand, colour, material, ISBN, platform, age rating) and the photo URLs.
- The photos of your own listing, to store them with the saved listing.

ReVint only acts on pages you open yourself. It does not scan other websites and does not collect data about other users.

## What ReVint stores
Saved listings, including photos as text (Base64), are stored locally on your device in the extension's own storage (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded. There is no account and no server.

## What ReVint shares
Nothing. The only network requests are to the Vinted pages and image URLs described above, made on your behalf.

## Retention and deletion
Delete saved listings at any time in the ReVint panel ("Delete selection"). Uninstalling the extension removes all data.

## Permissions
- `storage`: keeps your saved listings and the queue used to open several items in sequence.
- Host permissions for Vinted domains: to read your own listing pages and the sell form, and to load the images you uploaded to Vinted.

ReVint does not request permission to read other websites.

## Children
ReVint is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
